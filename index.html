⚙️ Data Generation Settings

Dispensing assumptions used:
• Daily OPD: 130–150 patients (avg 140) | Working days: 25/month
• Churnas / Kwatha Churnas: 50g packing | 25–30% patients prescribed
• Ghritas / Lehas: 150g jar | 25–30% patients prescribed
• Vati / Rasas: 20 pills/patient (10-day course) | 50% patients prescribed
• Tailas (100ml): 100ml bottle | 25–30% patients prescribed
• Tailas (10ml — Anu, Bilwa, Kumkumadi): 10ml bottle | 25–30% patients
• Guggulu: 20 tablets/patient | 50% patients prescribed
• Asava-Arishta: 200ml bottle | 25–30% patients prescribed
• Kashayas: 200ml bottle | 25–30% patients prescribed
• Syrups: 200ml bottle | 25–30% patients
• Liniments / External: 100ml/100g unit | 25–30% patients
• Opening stock = 90-day buffer based on monthly average

Formula used:
Daily dispensing (packs) = Working days × Avg daily patients × Prevalence % × 1 pack/patient
Monthly variation = Base × Seasonal factor × Random variation (±12%)
Opening stock = Monthly avg dispensing × (Buffer days ÷ 30)
PO quantity = Monthly avg dispensing × 2 months

Low stock trigger: Closing stock < (Daily rate × Min threshold days)
